# Changelog

## 0.2.0

- Added multi-level `bom` and `bomline` with component usage and scrap rate.
- Added `workorder`, `workorderline`, and `costentry` with BOM expansion on release, outbound stock/WIP issue costing, and inbound finished-goods receipt valued at issued component costs.
- Added idempotent `mrprun.execute` backward-scheduling `plannedworkorder` and `plannedrequisition` proposals with full demand/supply provenance and one-click confirmation into `purchasing.requisition` and `manufacturing.workorder`.
